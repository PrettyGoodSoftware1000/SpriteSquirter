# --- Personality traits ---------------------------------------------------------
# Every creature is born with these, and they last its whole life; Bless
# and Condemn train what it does on top of them. 100% is average. Each one
# multiplies a chance:
#   0%    never
#   50%   half as often as average
#   100%  average
#   150%  half as often again as average
#   200%  twice as often as average
#
# flee_personality: how readily it runs from a fight (it multiplies
#   flee_chance above, then its affinity for running at that health does
#   the rest). At 100%, untrained: 10% each 5-second check, so it usually
#   runs within about 50 seconds of fighting; at 200%: 20% (about 25 s);
#   at 50%: 5% (about 100 s); at 0% it never runs, however it is trained.
# mischief_personality: how readily it does mischief with the spells it
#   knows (it multiplies mischief_weight above). At 100%, untrained: about
#   once every 10 to 30 minutes for each spell it knows; at 1000%: every
#   1 to 3 minutes; at 0%: never.
# spellcaster_personality: how readily it casts any spell it knows, useful
#   or mischief (against spell_hold_weight). At 100%, with goblins to hit
#   and nothing trained: it casts about half the times it thinks of it
#   (every second); at 150%: 60%; at 50%: a third; at 0% it never casts.
# observant_personality: how readily it notices you casting a spell or the
#   villagers storing a resource, and learns from it (it multiplies
#   notice_chance above). At 100%: it notices 60% of the time; at 150%:
#   90%; at 50%: 30%. (It can never notice more than every time.)
# knowledgeSeeker_personality: the chance of learning extra when it does
#   notice something (it multiplies bonus_knowledge_chance). At 100%: 25%
#   of the time it learns twice as much; at 150%: 37.5%; at 0%: never. It
#   only speeds up learning new abilities: it does not change its
#   affinities, how Bless and Condemn work, or how likely it is to do
#   anything.
